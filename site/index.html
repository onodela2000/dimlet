WHY DIMLET EXISTS

The monitor is your charger.
But you don’t need its light.

“I only wanted to charge.”

Connect a USB-C monitor to your Mac and a picture appears too. Turning the monitor off may also stop USB-C power delivery, depending on the model.

“My Mac still has work to do.”

You want the screens dark while an AI agent, export, or long build keeps running. Putting the whole Mac to sleep doesn’t fit that task.

Dimlet keeps the display connection active and covers the screens with black windows. Choose external monitors only, or include the built-in screen. It prevents automatic idle sleep while the app is open.

Just charging, with no work to keep running? Your monitor’s standby charging settings or a separate charger may be enough. Dimlet is for a dark workspace while your Mac stays awake.

  1. 01

    Make a little room.

    Download the ZIP, unzip it, and move Dimlet to Applications. Open it to meet your new menu bar friend.

  2. 02

    Give your screens a break.

    Click the little monitor in your menu bar. Choose “Darken external monitors only” or “Darken all monitors (built-in + external)”. Select the active mode again to turn it off.

  3. 03

    Come back whenever.

    Click any black screen to bring them all back. Dimlet keeps your Mac awake until you quit the app.

THE LITTLE DETAILS

Good to know.

Can I keep USB-C charging while the monitor looks off?

Dimlet keeps the monitor powered and connected, so USB-C charging can continue if your hardware supports it. It makes the picture black; it does not disable the display signal or turn off the panel. Check your monitor’s power and standby charging settings first.

Does it actually turn my monitors off?

No. Dimlet covers your chosen screens with black windows. Keep the monitors physically on. It doesn't guarantee energy savings or a fully dark LCD backlight. USB-C charging depends on your hardware.

Can I close my MacBook?

Keep the lid open. Dimlet prevents automatic idle sleep while it runs; it doesn't override lid-close sleep, manual sleep, shutdown, or a depleted battery.

What about setup and permissions?

No Accessibility, Screen Recording, or admin permissions are needed to run Dimlet. The current release isn't Apple-notarized, so first launch may require System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. See the installation guide for details.

Will it work with my setup?

Dimlet supports macOS 13+. External-only mode skips mirrored screens to protect the built-in display; all-monitors mode covers all available screen surfaces. Tested on an M4 MacBook Air; Intel and older macOS versions still need broader real-world testing. The app menu supports English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, French, and German.
